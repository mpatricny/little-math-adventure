# Silverpond Town Hub Concept V1

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: complete 1280x720 static concept background for the main Silverpond TownScene hub in a side-view children's fantasy math adventure game

Primary request: Create the functional main town hub of Silverpond, not the arrival scene. It must contain all destinations from the original town as clearly separate, readable, clickable buildings: a general/adventurer shop, a guild, Pythia's witch workshop, a crystal forge/blacksmith, plus a distinct water gate on the far right that leads to a separate Water Arena screen.

Scene/backdrop: a magical freshwater lake settlement built on broad pale-stone quays and low islands, connected by short wooden bridges and canals. Forested hills, waterfalls and distant lake houses form the backdrop. The water is clear turquoise with faint submerged ancient ruins, but the foreground is a safe busy town plaza rather than wilderness.

Required layout, left to right:

1. Compact adventurer shop with teal roof, crates, shields and a sword emblem.
2. Taller civic guild hall with an open book-and-shield relief and broad welcoming door.
3. Whimsical Pythia workshop with curved purple-teal roof, crystal bottles, crescent and cauldron motifs.
4. Sturdy crystal forge/blacksmith with chimney, anvil-and-crystal relief, warm orange furnace glow and a small water wheel.
5. At the far right edge, a monumental arched water gate with flowing water, blue crystals and a path continuing off-screen toward the Water Arena. The gate is an exit, not a fifth full building.

Style/medium: polished hand-painted 2D fantasy game background, warm storybook charm, softly stylized shapes, painterly texture, clean readable silhouettes, child-friendly; visually consistent with the previously designed bright Silverpond arrival scene and with a lush painted RPG forest background. Not pixel art, not photorealistic, not a 3D render.

Composition/framing: wide 16:9 eye-level side-view game hub. Show all four complete building facades simultaneously, each with one obvious unobstructed entrance and generous visible separation. Buildings stand along one shared middle-ground baseline and must not overlap each other. Keep a broad, mostly level uninterrupted stone promenade across the entire lower third for the runtime player to walk horizontally. Reserve small clear spaces in front of every entrance for labels and interaction markers. Leave some calm sky in the upper center for runtime UI. The right-side water gate visibly continues to another screen.

Lighting/mood: bright late morning, welcoming after the forest journey, luminous lake reflections and warm windows; adventurous with a gentle aquatic mystery.

Color palette: turquoise and deep teal water, silver-blue stone, warm honey timber, teal roof shingles, restrained lavender crystal accents, orange forge glow. Distinct from the green-heavy Verdant Forest.

Materials/textures: weathered wood, pale lake stone, teal ceramic shingles, ropes, reeds, hammered metal, translucent crystals, softly rippling water.

Text: no text in the image.

Constraints: exactly four full functional buildings plus one right-edge arena gate. No characters and no creatures because player and NPCs will be runtime sprites. No embedded UI, no labels, no written signs, no numbers, no logo, no border, no watermark. Do not hide entrances behind props. Maintain stable readable geometry suitable for later splitting into separate interactive layers. Keep the foreground walking zone uncluttered.

Avoid: atmospheric establishing shot with only generic houses, a single giant central building, tightly packed architecture, overlapping buildings, generic Venice, ocean harbor, gloomy swamp, giant castle, steampunk machinery, isometric view, top-down view, warped doors, combat, corruption, photorealism, embedded text.
