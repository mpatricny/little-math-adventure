# Author correction — 19 November 2026

The author subsequently clarified that **forms 01 and 04 are accepted and must
remain unchanged**. Forms **02, 03 and 05** need new default poses and animation.
Those three rejected motions must not be integrated into the game. Earlier
technical reviews do not constitute approval of their posture or movement.

The evolving appearance of the fox is retained. Evolution does not assign a
different permanent body pose to each form. Every action should start and end in
a comfortable, anatomically plausible resting stance.

- Default: relaxed standing on four paws, natural canine back and weight support.
- Idle: breathing, blinking, ear and tail movement. A paw lift, stretch or sitting
  variation may occur occasionally, then return naturally to rest.
- Attack: ordinary short forward movement with weight transfer and a natural
  recovery. No fixed rear with a twisting or dramatically bent front half.
- Spell: subtle natural head/ear/body gesture; a separate light effect carries
  the magic. A lifted paw is not mandatory.
- Idle playback remains forward-only. No ping-pong or silhouette morphing.

Revised sources and motion tests for 02, 03 and 05 are separate version 2 artifacts.
The first neutral 01 sketch was exploratory and does not replace accepted V1 01.
Validate one revised fox before repeating its motion direction across the other
two. Keep the accepted light effect; healing, instant strike and an airy protective
shield belong on the recipient and are developed in the battle prototype.
