# Silverpond Fairy Reward – production prompts

All bitmap concepts and source layers were generated with the built-in image
generation tool. Runtime text is intentionally excluded from every generated UI
asset.

## Restored arena background

Edit the supplied Silverpond Water Arena 3 background while preserving its exact
camera, architecture, platform geometry, perspective, and composition. Remove
the storm, lightning, and threatening energy. Restore a calm blue-violet twilight
sky, moonlit clouds, tranquil waterfalls, steady turquoise crystal light, and
warm antique-gold accents. Keep the center platform empty. Background only: no
characters, enemies, UI, text, particles, crop, or zoom.

## Canonical Lake Fairy

Create exactly one complete freed Lake Fairy of Silverpond, hovering upright and
facing screen-left in a gentle three-quarter view. Use a kind adult face, pale
aqua hair, translucent water-fin wings, a pearl-and-antique-gold crown with one
central removable teardrop scale, deep-blue and lavender water-silk dress, and
gold-and-crystal jewelry. Match the Silverpond hand-painted fantasy game style.
Center the complete body with generous safety padding on a perfectly flat
`#00FF00` background. No green on the subject, no scenery, text, shadow, extra
anatomy, detached objects, or cropped tips.

## Water-breathing scale

Create exactly one isolated teardrop fish scale matching the Lake Fairy's crown:
cyan and ice-blue pearl-crystal material, luminous lavender core, delicate
antique-gold bezel, two curved scale ridges, and three compact droplets. Strong
readable silhouette, centered with generous padding on flat `#00FF00`. No fairy,
hand, scenery, UI, text, shadow, or green on the item.

## Silverpond story frame sheet

Create exactly two empty, vertically stacked production UI frames in one shared
Silverpond system: a compact wide title banner and a larger reward panel. Use
dark navy glass, antique gold, cyan crystal corners, restrained lavender pearls,
and subtle water-wave engraving. Large clear centers for runtime text. No icons,
text, characters, scenery, button, watermark, or cropped ornament. Isolate the
frames on transparency.

## Silverpond action-button pair

Create exactly two aligned copies of one empty Silverpond action-button frame in
equal side-by-side cells. Use the story-frame materials and a circular right-hand
socket containing a water-scale emblem. The active state changes only cyan edge
light, emblem glow, and compact droplets; silhouette, size, and placement remain
identical. Flat `#00FF00` background, no text, crop, shadow, or green on the UI.

## Sorceress animations

The complete production prompts for the one-second `idle` and `give` videos are
kept in `scripts/sorceress-lake-fairy-animations.sh`. They lock camera, scale,
center, anatomy, palette, chroma background, and safe padding. AutoSprite V3 uses
Every 4 sampling; `assemble-lake-fairy-production-sheets.py` selects the final
continuous action windows and exports WebP sheets.
